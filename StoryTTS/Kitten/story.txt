On a quiet morning, Milo the fox found a tiny brass bell beside the forest path.
It rang whenever the wind touched it, but no one knew who had left it there.

Milo carried the bell from tree to tree and asked every neighbor for help.
The owl listened carefully. The rabbit searched under the leaves. Even the old
turtle followed the sound, one patient step at a time.

At sunset, the bell rang again near a fallen log. Behind it, a little girl was
waiting with a worried face. She had lost the bell while looking for her family.
Milo led her through the glowing grass, and soon her parents called her name.

The girl smiled and tied the bell to Milo's favorite red scarf. From that day on,
the fox and the girl visited the forest together. Whenever the bell began to ring,
the whole woodland seemed to remember that a small act of kindness can guide
someone safely home.
